// about

Wallets, contracts & security.

I'm a blockchain developer from Ahmedabad, focused on the parts of Web3 where mistakes are expensive: private keys, token flows, and smart contracts that hold real money.

At Pedals Up I was a core engineer on Dlicom, a privacy-first SocialFi app with a self-custody wallet, social feed, creator tips, short video, communities, and a built-in dApp browser. It is live on Google Play and the App Store. I built its non-custodial multi-chain wallet and the Staking For Revenue (SFR) contracts, and I audited contracts with Slither, Aderyn, and manual review before release.

I also co-teach CSE 542: Blockchain Technologies at Ahmedabad University, and I build at hackathons, including a track win at ETHGlobal New Delhi.
